# UrsBiz Controlled MSME Pilot — Participant Informed Consent Form

**Document ID**: `UB-PILOT-CONSENT-2026-V1`  
**Application Platform**: UrsBiz (MSME Decision Support & Business Intelligence)  
**Study / Pilot Stage**: Prototype Engineering Verification & Real-World Feasibility Evaluation  

---

## 1. Purpose of the Pilot

You are invited to participate in a controlled feasibility pilot of **UrsBiz**, an AI-powered business intelligence and decision-support platform. The purpose of this pilot is to evaluate the usability, clarity, and practical relevance of UrsBiz's sales intelligence, demand estimation, and recommendation tools when applied to real micro, small, and medium enterprise (MSME) operations.

---

## 2. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw

Participation in this pilot is entirely voluntary:
- You may decline to participate without any disadvantage or penalty.
- You have the right to withdraw from the pilot at any time, for any reason.
- Upon withdrawal, you may request the immediate and permanent deletion of your business profile, uploaded sales records, and generated recommendations from the UrsBiz pilot databases.

---

## 3. Data Collection & Intended Use

If you agree to participate, the UrsBiz pilot team will collect only data strictly necessary for platform operation:
1. **Business Profile**: Business name, industry category, enterprise tier (Micro/Small/Medium), year established, operational city/state, and basic compliance indicators (e.g. whether registered under GST or MSME Udyam).
2. **Sales & Transaction Data**: Historical sales transactions uploaded via CSV or Excel (e.g. order date, product name/SKU, quantity sold, unit price, optional customer identifier).
3. **Pilot Evaluation Data**: Answers to onboarding checklists, weekly check-ins, issue reports, and pilot completion surveys.

### Permitted Uses:
- Ingesting and processing your data within your dedicated UrsBiz account.
- Generating sales analytics, demand estimations, and personalized business recommendations for your review.
- Evaluating system usability, model reliability, and platform performance.

### Explicit Prohibitions:
- **No Unrelated Use**: Your data will never be sold, rented, or repurposed for commercial advertising or third-party marketing.
- **No Public AI Training**: Your confidential business records will never be used to train foundational public LLM models.
- **No Cross-Business Leakage**: Your data is strictly partitioned and inaccessible to any other business or pilot participant.
- **No Public Disclosure**: In any academic, research, or public engineering reports, all findings will be strictly aggregated or anonymized. No proprietary sales figures, product margins, or business identities will ever be published.

---

## 4. Operational Boundaries & Disclaimers

By participating, you acknowledge and agree to the following operational boundaries:

1. **Decision-Support Only**: UrsBiz provides analytical decision-support tools. Recommendations generated by the platform do **not** constitute certified financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. You remain solely responsible for any commercial decisions or operational changes you choose to execute.
2. **Demand Estimates, Not Guarantees**: Demand forecasts are statistical projections based on historical data. They do not account for unforeseen external events, macroeconomic disruptions, or supply-chain shocks, and must not be treated as guaranteed future demand.
3. **No Inventory Optimization**: UrsBiz does not currently model supplier lead times, minimum order quantities (MOQs), safety stocks, or holding costs. The platform provides demand projections only, not validated automated inventory optimization.
4. **AI Output Verification**: AI-generated explanations, summaries, and action plans are generated using generative AI grounded in your uploaded data. AI models can occasionally produce inaccuracies or omissions; you agree to review all generated explanations before acting on them.
5. **Request-Driven Monitoring**: System monitoring, health updates, and recommendation generation operate upon data upload and user request. UrsBiz does not currently provide 24/7 autonomous background monitoring or emergency alerts.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage Commitments

- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and encrypted at rest within secure PostgreSQL cloud databases.
- **Role-Based Access**: Access to database records is restricted to authenticated user accounts and designated pilot engineers bound by confidentiality obligations.
- **Retention & Deletion**: Your pilot data will be retained for the duration of the 4-week pilot and up to 30 days post-pilot for data export, after which all operational records can be purged upon your written request.
