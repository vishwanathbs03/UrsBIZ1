---

### Severity Level *(Check one)*:
- [ ] **Critical (Blocker)**: Cannot log in, upload data fails completely, or data corruption/loss. Pilot cannot proceed.
- [ ] **High**: A major feature is failing (e.g. recommendations do not generate), but other modules work.
- [ ] **Medium**: Feature works, but has a calculation discrepancy, confusing UI, or requires a workaround.
- [ ] **Low**: Minor visual defect, typographical error, or improvement suggestion.
