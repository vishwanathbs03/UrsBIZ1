# UrsBiz MSME Controlled Pilot — Invitation Letter

**Dear Business Owner / General Manager,**

We are pleased to invite your business to participate in an exclusive, controlled real-world pilot of **UrsBiz**, an AI-powered business intelligence and decision-support platform specifically designed for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

---

## 1. What is UrsBiz?

UrsBiz is an intelligent business advisor built to help growing enterprises turn everyday sales records into clear, actionable business decisions. Instead of complex, expensive enterprise software, UrsBiz provides:
- **Sales & Revenue Intelligence**: Clear visibility into your top products, sales trends, revenue distribution, and customer purchase patterns.
- **Demand Forecasting**: Data-driven estimates of upcoming customer demand to assist with seasonal planning and capacity budgeting.
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Context-aware suggestions tailored to your business tier, industry, and current health score across growth, cash flow, operational efficiency, and digital adoption.
- **Outcome Tracking**: A guided workflow to track actions from acceptance to completion and assess business changes over time.

---

## 2. Why a Controlled Pilot?

UrsBiz has successfully passed extensive engineering and integration testing. However, **real-world business value and user experience can only be validated with real business owners like you**. 

We are conducting this small, focused pilot with **3 to 5 selected MSMEs** to:
1. Validate how easily business owners can upload sales data and navigate the platform.
2. Determine whether our demand estimates and business insights are relevant and practical in real daily operations.
3. Understand which recommendations owners find genuinely useful and feasible to implement.
4. Identify gaps, bugs, or workflow frictions before general availability.

---

## 3. What Does Participation Involve?

As a pilot participant, you will receive:
- **Free, Dedicated Access**: Full access to the UrsBiz platform for your business throughout the pilot period.
- **Personalized Onboarding Support**: One-on-one guided assistance to set up your business profile and format your initial sales records.
- **Custom Business Health Assessment**: An automated evaluation of your business profile, compliance readiness, and digital footprint.
- **Direct Line to the Engineering Team**: Rapid resolution of any questions, feedback, or issues.

### Time Commitment:
- **Week 1 (Onboarding)**: 45 minutes for profile setup and initial sales data upload.
- **Weeks 2 & 3 (Weekly Check-in)**: 15 minutes per week to review generated recommendations and complete a short 5-question feedback form.
- **Week 4 (Pilot Review)**: 20 minutes for a pilot wrap-up interview or questionnaire.

---

## 4. Participant Criteria

To ensure mutual value, we are prioritizing businesses that meet the following criteria:
- [x] Operating enterprise in retail, manufacturing, distribution, food & beverage, or services.
- [x] Availability of historical sales records (minimum 3 to 6 months of daily, weekly, or monthly transaction data in Excel, CSV, or billing exports).
- [x] Clear product or service catalog (identifiable product names or SKUs).
- [x] An active business owner or operational manager willing to review insights and share honest feedback.
- [x] Full legal authority and permission to upload business sales data for the purpose of this pilot.

---

## 5. Important Pilot Boundaries & Disclaimers

UrsBiz is committed to complete transparency regarding its current prototype stage:
- **Decision Support, Not Advice**: UrsBiz provides decision support and analytical estimates. It does not provide certified financial, accounting, tax, or legal advice.
- **Demand Estimates, Not Guarantees**: Demand projections are statistical estimates based on historical patterns; they are not guarantees of future customer orders.
- **No Inventory Optimization Claims**: While UrsBiz provides demand estimates, it does not currently model supplier lead times, stockout risks, or warehouse buffer stocks.
- **AI Explanations Require Review**: Explanations generated by artificial intelligence are grounded in your data, but must always be reviewed by you before executing business actions.
- **Request-Driven Monitoring**: System monitoring and recommendation generation run upon data uploads and explicit user requests, rather than as an unmonitored background automated system.

---

## 6. How Your Data is Protected

Your business data is strictly protected:
- **Tenant Isolation**: Your data is logically separated and only accessible to your authenticated account.
- **Strictly Limited Purpose**: Your data will only be used to generate your business insights and evaluate pilot usability.
- **No AI Model Training on Private Data**: Your confidential sales records are never used to train public machine learning models.
- **Full Control & Deletion**: You may request complete export or deletion of your business data at any time.
