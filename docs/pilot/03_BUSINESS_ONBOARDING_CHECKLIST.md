## Phase A: Pre-Onboarding Eligibility & Verification

*Completed prior to platform account registration.*

- [ ] **Eligibility Criteria Check**:
  - [ ] Business is an active MSME (Micro, Small, or Medium enterprise).
  - [ ] Has identifiable physical products or discrete commercial services.
  - [ ] Has at least 3 to 6 months of historical transaction/sales records.
- [ ] **Authority & Consent**:
  - [ ] Participant Informed Consent Form (`02_PARTICIPANT_CONSENT_FORM.md`) signed by authorized representative.
  - [ ] Contact details and preferred feedback channel (Email/WhatsApp) confirmed.
- [ ] **Confidentiality Confirmation**:
  - [ ] Pilot coordinator provided written confirmation of data isolation and deletion guarantees.

---

## Phase B: Account Setup & Authentication

*Platform URL*: `https://ursbiz-frontend.onrender.com/`

- [ ] **User Registration**:
  - [ ] Navigate to `/register`.
  - [ ] Enter owner full name, authorized email address, and strong password (min. 8 characters, alphanumeric).
  - [ ] Submit registration and verify successful account creation.
- [ ] **Initial Login**:
  - [ ] Navigate to `/login`.
  - [ ] Log in with created credentials; verify redirection to the main dashboard.
  - [ ] Verify that session token is securely stored and user profile loads cleanly.

---

## Phase C: Business Profile Configuration

*Complete the 3-step Business Setup wizard in UrsBiz.*

- [ ] **Step 1: Core Entity Information**:
  - [ ] Enter Official Business Legal Name and Trade Name.
  - [ ] Select primary Industry Sector (e.g. Retail, Food & Beverage, Light Manufacturing, Apparel, Services, Hardware).
  - [ ] Specify Year Established and Operating Location (City, State, Pincode).
- [ ] **Step 2: MSME Tier & Scale**:
  - [ ] Select Enterprise Classification based on annual turnover and investment:
    - Micro (Turnover $\le$ ₹5 Cr)
    - Small (Turnover ₹5 Cr – ₹50 Cr)
    - Medium (Turnover ₹50 Cr – ₹250 Cr)
  - [ ] Enter approximate number of full-time and part-time employees.
- [ ] **Step 3: Compliance & Digital Footprint**:
  - [ ] Indicate GSTIN registration status (Registered / Unregistered / Composition).
  - [ ] Indicate MSME Udyam registration status (Registered / Pending / Unregistered).
  - [ ] Indicate current digital presence channels (Physical Storefront only, WhatsApp Business, E-Commerce Website, Social Media, Online Marketplaces like Amazon/Flipkart/Swiggy/Zomato).
  - [ ] Save Business Profile and verify that the business appears as active in the header selector.

## Phase D: Baseline Health Assessment

- [ ] Navigate to the **Business Profile / Dashboard** view.
- [ ] Inspect the automatically generated **Business Health Score** ($0-100$ scale).
- [ ] Review breakdown across:
  - Regulatory & Formalization Readiness
  - Digital Presence & Channel Maturity
  - Profile Completeness
- [ ] Note baseline score on the Participant Tracking Sheet.

## Phase E: Technical Environment Validation

- [ ] Verify browser compatibility:
  - Supported: Google Chrome (v110+), Mozilla Firefox (v115+), Microsoft Edge (v110+), Safari (v16+).
- [ ] Verify display layout:
  - Recommended desktop/laptop resolution: $1280 \times 720$ or higher.
  - Verify that navigation sidebar, header, and data cards render without overlapping.
- [ ] Bookmark application URL on participant's browser.
